On `launcher="submitit_slurm"` or `"submitit_local"`, a job that ends without returning after its cell has completed (its result reached the driver too late, or, in a job of several tasks, another task died after the first one returned) can now keep the cell's result rather than become a failed cell. A cell's `mushin_cell_status.json` now records the run that wrote it and the job it ran in (`run_id`, `job_id`), which is how mushin tells the job's own record from one an earlier run left in the directory: a dead job no longer leaves such a record standing for its cell, unless it is a completed result of the same cell under the same config and code, which a resume reuses.
