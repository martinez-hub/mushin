On `launcher="submitit_slurm"` or `"submitit_local"`, a job that ends without a result (killed at its time limit, out of memory, or on a failed node) no longer aborts a sweep run with `on_error="nan"`. Its cell is NaN and recorded as failed, as the multinode guide says, and the cell's `mushin_error.txt` names the job's log. Under the default `on_error="raise"` the error is raised once the jobs launched with it have ended, rather than at the first such job while the others go on unobserved. `launcher="submitit_slurm"` and `"submitit_local"` (or the same `hydra/launcher=` override) now run on mushin's subclass of the plugin's launcher, so a cell's `.hydra/hydra.yaml` names `mushin_submitit_slurm` (or `mushin_submitit_local`).
