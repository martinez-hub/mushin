`mushin.show`, `mushin.best`, `mushin.diff` and the CSV export no longer list an earlier sweep's record and metrics for a cell whose job died after Hydra wrote its `.hydra/` snapshot (in the default `.hydra` folder) and before the cell recorded anything, in a directory a later sweep reused: they leave that cell out, where `load_from_dir` counts it as not run, or failed when its own run's manifest says so. As for the reload, a resume interrupted in a cell between Hydra's snapshot and the cell's record leaves that cell out until the next resume reuses it.
