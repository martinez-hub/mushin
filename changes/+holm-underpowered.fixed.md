The underpowered-test warning of `compare_methods`, `compare` and `Study` now accounts for the correction over the method pairs: under Holm or Bonferroni the smallest p-value must clear `alpha / m` for `m` pairs, so Wilcoxon needs 7 seeds for 3 methods and 8 for 4, not 6, and the warning says so instead of staying silent while no pair can be significant. Pairs masked for a constant method are outside the family, and `warn_if_underpowered` takes the family size and the correction as optional arguments.
