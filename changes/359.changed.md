On `launcher="submitit_slurm"` or `"submitit_local"`, a resume now submits jobs only for the cells it must compute, `sample=` only for the cells it keeps, and a `cache_dir` hit no job at all: the driver settles the others before anything is queued, recording each as its job would have. A resume that fills 3 cells of 1,000 queues 3 jobs, not 1,000. A cell the driver cannot judge as its job would (a config it cannot resolve, or one that reads `${oc.env:...}`, whose environment the job's may not match) is still submitted, and its job decides as before; so is every cell of a sweep with Hydra callbacks, which run in each job.
