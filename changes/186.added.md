New `examples/inspect_ai_compare.py` answers the question [Inspect AI](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk) leaves open — your eval says one model scored 60% and another 56.7%, but is that gap real? It checks whether the difference survives a re-run (Inspect epochs) and whether it would survive a different set of questions (an item bootstrap), and reports a plain verdict. Run `--demo` to see it on two scenarios with known ground truth. Questions are matched across logs by `sample.id` rather than by position, since matching positionally would compare unrelated questions.
