New `examples/inspect_ai_compare.py` and an LLM-guide section showing how to feed [Inspect AI](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk) eval logs into `compare_scores`: Inspect samples become items and Inspect epochs become runs, so an `--epochs 5` eval gives mushin both dimensions. Samples are aligned by `sample.id` rather than by position, since pairing positionally would silently compare unrelated questions.
