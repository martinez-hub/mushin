A cell swept over a non-finite value (`inf`, `-inf`, NaN) is now read back as that value from its status record, where it is stored tagged or null: `mushin.show`, `best`, `diff` and the CSV export list the finished cell (`show` dropped a swept `inf` once its sweep finished), its resume finds its own checkpoint, and mushin's submitit launchers keep its result when its job's return is lost.
