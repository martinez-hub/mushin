A cell that runs in a directory another cell used (a sweep over another grid in the same `working_dir`) is no longer handed that cell's checkpoint as `mushin_resume.last_ckpt`: when its record replaces the other cell's, it marks the checkpoints there (their names, and the newest time among them), and a checkpoint so marked and no newer is never handed to it, on a resume, a plain re-run of a completed cell, or a `sample=` fill-in, where it had resumed from the other cell's weights. Nothing is moved or deleted. A directory taken over by an earlier version, and a checkpoint in a subfolder, are not covered.
