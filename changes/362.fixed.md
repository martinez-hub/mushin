A reload (`load_from_dir`) no longer takes an earlier sweep's record and metrics for a cell whose job died after Hydra wrote its `.hydra/` snapshot and before the cell recorded anything, in a directory a later sweep reused: a record older than that snapshot, from another run than the one the snapshot names, is not the cell's. The cell is NaN and counts as not run, or failed when its own run's manifest says so. A sweep copied without its files' times keeps every result.
