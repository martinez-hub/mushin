`mushin.cancel(working_dir, dry_run=False)` cancels a SLURM sweep's jobs that are still queued or running, from a login node, with the driver running or not, and returns their ids. It reads the submissions the driver recorded under `<working_dir>/mushin_jobs/`, asks `scontrol` about each job array, and runs `scancel` once. A job is cancelled only when its script or logs lie under the sweep's submitit folder, so a job id SLURM has since given another job is left alone, with a warning. When `scontrol` cannot be read for a job, nothing is cancelled and the error names the `scancel` command to run by hand. `submitit_local` jobs, and jobs sent to another cluster with `--clusters`, are skipped with a warning (#345).
