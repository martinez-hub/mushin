`run()` now refuses a `working_dir` whose earlier jobs, submitted by `launcher="submitit_slurm"` or `"submitit_local"`, may still be queued or running, whatever launcher the new run uses, where it went ahead before: a resume started while a killed driver's jobs still ran submitted their cells again, and two jobs then wrote one cell. It raises a `ValueError` naming the submission's file under `mushin_jobs/` for a job SLURM lists as not ended (pending, running, or preempted, which SLURM may requeue), or a local job whose process runs; wait for the jobs to end, or cancel them (`mushin.cancel`), then run again. It raises a `RuntimeError` when `scontrol` is found but gives no answer it can read, and for jobs sent to another cluster (`--clusters`), which plain `scontrol` does not see. Where the jobs cannot be checked (no `scontrol`, or `submitit_local` jobs of another machine), it warns and goes ahead. A run that goes ahead removes the files of the submissions it found over. `mushin.show` and `mushin.cancel` now also count a `submitit_local` job whose process has exited but was not reaped (a zombie) as ended.
