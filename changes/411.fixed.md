On `launcher="submitit_slurm"` or `"submitit_local"`, a cell whose job ended without a result no longer takes as its own a record of the same run and job that names another cell's job number, and `mushin.show` no longer takes such a record as a sign that the cell's job started. This matters where cells share a job id, as the cells of one job that runs several in turn do: the cell took the other cell's result, and `show` listed no row for the cells the job had not reached. A record with no job number is still judged by its job id alone.
