A cell that runs in a directory another cell used (a sweep over another grid in the same `working_dir`) is no longer handed that cell's checkpoint as `mushin_resume.last_ckpt`: when its record replaces the other cell's, it lists the checkpoints at the directory's top, and a resume of it skips them while they are unchanged. On a resume, on a plain re-run of a completed cell, and on a `sample=` fill-in, it had resumed from the other cell's weights. Nothing is moved or deleted. A directory taken over by an earlier version, and a checkpoint in a subfolder, are not covered.
