`mushin.slurm(gpus=, cpus=, mem_gb=, timeout_min=, partition=, account=, qos=, setup=)` builds the `launcher_config` of a sweep whose cells each run as one SLURM job of one task, for `launcher="submitit_slurm"`, without importing Lightning; `mushin.submitit_slurm_config` stays the helper for multi-node DDP, whose jobs run a task per GPU. Where `scontrol` answers, it refuses a partition that does not exist or a `timeout_min` past the partition's time limit before anything is submitted, naming the partitions that fit (`check=False` skips it); it never picks a partition, and leaves GPUs and accounts to SLURM.
