On `launcher="submitit_slurm"` or `"submitit_local"`, `launcher_config={"cells_per_job": k}` (or `mushin.slurm(..., cells_per_job=k)`) runs up to k of a sweep's cells in each job, one after another in one Python process, so a sweep of many short cells costs fewer jobs and stays under the cluster's job-array and queue limits. Cells are grouped in submission order, after a resume, `sample=` or `cache_dir` has set aside the cells that need no job. Each cell keeps its own directory, record, metrics file and `.hydra/` snapshot, a cell that raises does not stop its job, and a requeued job skips the cells it finished. `timeout_min` is the whole job's limit. When a job ends without returning, the cells it finished keep their results when their records hold them, and the cell it was running and those it had not reached fail, each with the job's error; a resume runs them again. `mushin.show` lists the cells a running job has not reached as `queued`, with its job id. Several tasks per job (`tasks_per_node` or `nodes` above 1) and a plain Hydra task function are refused before anything is submitted, and `run()` refuses a `cells_per_job` key in `launcher_config` for any other launcher. mushin's launcher config is the plugin's plus this one field, which defaults to 1, one cell per job, as before.
