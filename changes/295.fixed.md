For a task defined in a notebook, `resume=True` and `cache_dir=` now compare a class with no method of its own (a dataclass or an `Enum` of fields, a class of constants) by its `class` statement, and a class whose every method a notebook decorator wraps. In a running IPython shell mushin notes which cell each class statement ran from; a class whose cell was edited before that, or one a factory makes, is found among the kept cells, and when several of their texts could be it, its cells re-run instead of reusing results. A resume re-runs the cells once after upgrading for a task that reaches such a class.
