`run(..., wait=False)` on `launcher="submitit_slurm"` or `"submitit_local"` submits the sweep's jobs and returns at once, a dict of the `working_dir`, the run's id, the number of cells submitted and the job ids, so the driver no longer has to stay alive in `tmux` or an `sbatch` job while they queue and run. Once they have ended, the new `mushin.collect(working_dir)` (or the workflow's `collect()`) finishes the run as a waiting `run()` would have, from any Python session: a cell whose job ended without a result is recorded failed, the manifest is written, and the dataset is returned. It raises, writing nothing, while any job is queued or running. `wait=False` needs a `working_dir` and Hydra's basic sweeper. A `run()` into the `working_dir` of a run submitted this way is refused until the run is collected or its jobs have ended, and, where those jobs cannot be checked, raises a `RuntimeError` instead of only warning. **Breaking** for a sweep whose task, `pre_task` or config has its own `wait`: `run(wait=...)` used to pass the value to the task, and now raises a `ValueError` for such a sweep, whatever the value. Pass it as an override instead: `run(overrides=["++wait=5"], ...)` in place of `run(wait=5, ...)`.
