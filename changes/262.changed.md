`compare_llms` and `compare_scores` compute their item-level bootstrap about 2.7x faster (5 systems x 5,000 items: 0.6 s instead of 1.6 s; 10 systems: 2.4 s instead of 7.4 s), with results identical to the last digit. The pairs of one metric resample the same eval set, so their indices, which took most of the time, are now drawn once and shared. `paired_item_bootstrap` is unchanged; the new `paired_item_bootstraps` does the batch.
