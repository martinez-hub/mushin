In an IPython shell, mushin's cell watch now walks the live classes once per cell instead of twice: a top-level cell starts from the classes the previous one ended with. With torch, Lightning, numpy, pandas and xarray loaded, that cuts the watch's cost from about 16 ms to about 8 ms per cell on an Apple M1. A class a background thread or a callback makes between two cells may now be taken for one the next cell made, as one made while a cell runs already could.
